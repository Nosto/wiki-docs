# Recommendations for Shopify Checkout & Thank You Pages

## Setting Up in Nosto <a href="#h_c09402ad35" id="h_c09402ad35"></a>

For all eligible merchants, Nosto automatically creates default recommendations for Shopify Checkout and Thank You pages. However, you should review the settings to ensure they align with your business needs. To do this:

1. Log into your Nosto account.
2. Go to Product Experience Cloud → Recommendations.
3. Check the pre-created recommendations for Shopify Checkout and Thank You Page.
4. Customize the recommendation settings to use algorithms that best match your business strategy.
5. Save and Publish any updates.

Your recommendations are now optimized and ready to be placed in Shopify!

## Setting Up in Shopify <a href="#h_207ef6aa52" id="h_207ef6aa52"></a>

To display Nosto recommendations on Shopify Checkout and Thank You pages, follow these steps:

1. Log into Shopify Admin.
2. Go to Settings > Checkout.
3. Click Customize to open the Theme Editor.
4. Add Nosto App Blocks:
   * Nosto Pre-Purchase (for Checkout Page)
   * Nosto Thank-You (for Thank You Page)
5. Drag and drop the blocks into your preferred location.
6. Save and Publish your changes.

That’s it! Your Nosto recommendations are now live on your Shopify Checkout and Thank You pages.

## Summary <a href="#h_0f0fe83128" id="h_0f0fe83128"></a>

By integrating Nosto into your Shopify Checkout and Thank You pages, you can create a personalized shopping experience that boosts conversions. While recommendations are automatically created, reviewing and adjusting the settings ensures they align with your business goals. Simply check your recommendations in Nosto, place the app blocks in Shopify, and you’re good to go! For further assistance reach out to our support team or your Customer Success Manager.
